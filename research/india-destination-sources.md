# India Destination Catalogue Sources

TripWise’s expanded explorer will cover a curated set of major travel destinations across **all 28 Indian states and 8 union territories**, while making clear that a software catalogue cannot enumerate every locality, attraction, and seasonal event in the country.

The National Portal of India identifies India as having 28 states and 8 union territories. [1]

Incredible India’s public discovery portal organizes travel discovery by geographic region—North, North East, East, Central, West, and South—and by experiences such as adventure, nature, wildlife, heritage, spiritual, wellness, arts, rural, and recreation. [2]
